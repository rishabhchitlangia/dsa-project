Seventy‑Eight

A reading table · 78 cards · Rider–Waite–Smith tradition

Bring a question to the cards.

Shuffle the deck yourself, cut it in three, and pick your cards by hand from the fan. The reading weighs each card by its position, its orientation and the cards around it.

Open questions work best: “what”, “how”, “what do I need to know”. Leave it blank for a general reading.
